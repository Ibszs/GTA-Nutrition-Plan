# Asset register

Everything Forme ships is stored in this repository and served from it. The app makes no request to a font service, an icon service, an image host or a video site. Outside links (creator recipes, studies, demo videos) are plain links that open only when tapped.

The same register is shown inside the app at **You, Credits and sources**, generated from `app/data/credits.js`, so the two cannot drift apart. Retrieved 16 to 17 Nov 2026.

## Drawn plates (since 5.0.0)

Two recipes have no photograph because no properly licensed picture matched the dish: the hot honey beef and sweet potato bowl, and the chicken and hummus pita box. Each has an illustration instead, drawn in code in `app/plates.js` from the recipe's own ingredients and how it is served. They are original to Forme, there is no outside artwork in them, and no file was downloaded for them. In the app each one is labelled "Illustration" and its recipe page explains why it is a drawing.

Version 5.0.0 drew four plates. Version 5.0.1 replaced two of them (smash burger bowl, cheeseburger pasta) with the photographs listed above. For the other two, a search of Openverse and Wikimedia Commons on 19 Nov 2026 found nothing close enough: the nearest for the pita box were a child's lunchbox with crackers and strawberries and a hot chicken and hummus plate, and nothing showed ground beef with sweet potato and cottage cheese.

## Gym photographs in more places (since 5.0.0)

The 76 Free Exercise DB photographs listed under "Everything else" in the app are now also used as thumbnails and as a two-frame demo (start and finish, crossfaded) on Train. No photograph was added, changed or re-cropped; the rule that a lift only gets a picture when the photographs match that exact variation is unchanged.

Notes on fit, also shown in the app beside the photo:

- `shake.jpg` shows a blueberry smoothie. The house shake is paler because of the oats and peanut butter.
- `wrap.jpg` shows the wrap served with fries. The fries are not part of the recipe.
- `store.jpg` shows rotisserie chicken with rice and beans, standing in for a grocery-store plate. The recipe lists its own three items.

Four recipes have no photograph: Smash Burger Bowl, Hot Honey Beef and Sweet Potato Bowl, One-Pot Cheeseburger Pasta, and the No-Cook Chicken and Hummus Pita Box. No properly licensed picture that actually matched the dish was found, so each shows a designed card in its own colour with the dish's one-line description. The share-alike licences on four photographs cover those photographs only.

## Gym movement photographs

Seventy-six unmodified JPEGs under `assets/images/exercises/` come from [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), which describes its data and imagery as public domain. The upstream [Unlicense](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/a859101d633a01c4a1a920d6a8ce41dabba0705f/LICENSE.md) is kept in `docs/licenses/free-exercise-db.txt`. Pinned source revision: `a859101d633a01c4a1a920d6a8ce41dabba0705f`.

These were audited for the previous version (see `docs/exercise-audit-2026.md`) and are carried over unchanged. `assets/exercise-visuals.js` maps each audited variation to its two photographs. A variation with no exact match shows text guidance, never a picture of different equipment. The photographs show two positions, not a required range of motion.

## Movement illustrations

The jointed figure, its 18 pairs of poses and the target overlays are original to Forme. They are drawn in code by `app/figure.js` from pose data in `app/data/exercises.js`: solid red marks what is working, blue stripes mark what is lengthening, and each target carries a number that matches the legend, so colour is never the only signal. Every drawing is labelled as an illustrative diagram in the app. No outside artwork is used.

## Icons

`assets/icons.svg` is one local sprite of 63 [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icons under the ISC licence (notice in `docs/licenses/lucide.txt`):

- 42 carried over from the previous version, from revision `94e4cb9d9db5907053ebf3636a97c45529cf776b` of lucide-icons/lucide.
- 21 added for Forme from `lucide-static` 1.73.0, stored under the short ids the app uses: `figure` (person-standing), `minus`, `trash` (trash-2), `pencil`, `more` (ellipsis), `grip` (grip-vertical), `arrow-up`, `arrow-down`, `arrow-left`, `snow` (snowflake), `fridge` (refrigerator), `receipt` (receipt-text), `film`, `star`, `lock`, `bell`, `bolt` (zap), `door` (door-open), `dice` (dices), `oil` (droplet), `ext` (external-link).

The app icon (`icons/icon-192.png`, `icons/icon-512.png`) was drawn for Forme: a heavy italic F in black on the pink stock with a red slanted rule under it, set in Sofia Sans Extra Condensed and rendered from HTML on 17 Nov 2026. It replaces the earlier generated icon.

## Typefaces

One superfamily in three widths, SIL Open Font License 1.1, bundled as WOFF2 in `assets/fonts/`. Designed by Lettersoup (Botio Nikoltchev and Ani Petrova). Licence text: `docs/licenses/sofia-sans.txt`.

| File | Family | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `sofia-sans-extra-condensed.woff2`, `sofia-sans-extra-condensed-italic.woff2` | Sofia Sans Extra Condensed (variable weight, upright and italic) | Headlines, buttons, dish names, clocks and big numerals |
| `sofia-sans-condensed.woff2` | Sofia Sans Condensed (variable weight) | Figures, tables, labels |
| `sofia-sans.woff2`, `sofia-sans-italic.woff2` | Sofia Sans (variable weight, upright and italic) | Everything you read |

All five are the Latin subsets served by Google Fonts (`fonts.gstatic.com`), downloaded 17 Nov 2026 and not modified. They replace Archivo, Young Serif, Martian Mono and DSEG7 from the previous design pass.

## Nutrition values

Per 100 g values come from USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy release (April 2018), which is public domain. Each food in `app/data/foods.js` carries its FDC id, and every recipe page lists each ingredient's contribution with a link to its record. Whey uses the label you enter.

## Removed in this version

These shipped with Form & Fuel 3.0 and are gone, because nothing in Forme uses them: the three generated editorial JPEGs, the Three.js runtime and the 3D dumbbell scene, Barlow Condensed and Manrope, `assets/kitchen.svg`, and the old page stylesheets and scripts. Their licence notices went with them. Git history still has every file.
