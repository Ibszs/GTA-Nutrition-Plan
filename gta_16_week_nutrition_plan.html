GTA 16-Week Scale-Target Nutrition Plan

Mobile-first weekly template for a 21-year-old, 6 ft 3 in, 168-172 lb active worker lifting in the evening. Built for the 190-195 lb week-16 scale target while reporting, not hiding, the body-composition cost.

Research and price access date: September 3, 2026. All money CAD. Whey label calories/carbohydrate/fat remain intentionally blank until read from the tub.

1. Reality-check the goal, then plan to it anyway

+18 to +27 lbRequired total scale gain
1.13-1.69 lb/wkRequired weekly pace
0.65-1.00%Current body weight per week
190-195 lbScale target has precedence
Plain reality: off-season evidence commonly favours roughly 0.25-0.5% of body weight per week for more favourable body-composition outcomes. At 170 lb that is about 0.43-0.85 lb/week. Your midpoint target is about 1.41 lb/week, so it is materially faster and its upper end is roughly double the favourable range.

Likely composition

  • Some gain can be muscle, particularly with progressive training and sufficient protein.
  • Early gain will also include glycogen, associated water, sodium-related fluid and more food in the gut.
  • At the requested pace, meaningful fat gain is likely. It is not credible to label 18-27 lb as lean tissue.
  • No percentage is assigned to water/glycogen/gut fill because the prompt data cannot support one.

Tracking signals

  • Weight: use a 7-day rolling average only.
  • Waist baseline: day one, measure at the navel three times under the same conditions and average.
  • Waist flag: baseline + 4.0 cm. This is an operational report threshold, not a clinical cutoff.
  • Important: the waist flag never cuts calories. Only a projected finish above 195 lb can do that.

Planned ending, not improvised ending

After week 16, hold the achieved intake for 7-14 days while the scale settles, then run a 10-16 week cut using an initial 400-600 kcal/day reduction, targeting roughly 0.5-0.75% of body weight per week. Keep lifting and keep protein near 180-200 g/day. Remove the same +250 modules used during the gain phase before rewriting meals.

Evidence basis: Iraki et al., off-season bodybuilding review.

2. Calories, protein and intake-response tracking

Maintenance estimate - estimate only

Mifflin-St Jeor RMR, midpoint 77 kg: 10 x 77 + 6.25 x 190.5 - 5 x 21 + 5 = 1,861 kcal/day Active-job/training factor 1.70-1.80: 1,861 x 1.70-1.80 = about 3,163-3,349 kcal/day

The formula suggests about 3,100-3,400 kcal maintenance. Your recalled 2,200-2,800 kcal intake with static weight points lower, but the recall is untracked. Neither estimate is validated. This plan resolves the mismatch by feeding a known amount immediately and measuring the response.

Provisional day-one intake: 3,300 kcal/day. This is an appetite-limited start, not a promise that 3,300 will be enough for the final target. Based on the formula, the full target may require 3,550, 3,800 or another +250 step. The tracker decides.

Whey-label correction - makes 3,300 exact

The template before whey is 3,186 kcal, 151.7 g protein, 425.3 g carbohydrate, 100.6 g fat and 36.6 g fibre. Add one scoop of whey:

Uncorrected calories = 3,186 + Wkcal Difference D = 3,300 - (3,186 + Wkcal) New breakfast honey = 20 g + D / 3.04
  • Keep the 15 g pre-workout honey unchanged.
  • If calculated breakfast honey is between 5 and 40 g, use it.
  • If it falls outside that range, keep breakfast honey at 20 g and adjust olive oil by D / 8.84 grams instead.
  • Example only: if the tub says 120 kcal/scoop, D = -6, so breakfast honey becomes about 18 g. The tub label remains authoritative.

Protein target

Target 175 g/day; acceptable operating band 165-185 g/day. At 76-78 kg, 175 g is about 2.24-2.30 g/kg now and about 1.98-2.03 g/kg at 190-195 lb. This is a transparent extrapolation from resistance-trained evidence, not an enhanced-specific formula. ISSN identifies 1.4-2.0 g/kg/day as sufficient for most exercising people, and bodybuilding literature commonly works around 1.6-2.2 g/kg/day. The menu sits slightly above the upper current-weight edge so the target still works after body weight rises.

Evidence: ISSN protein position stand and off-season bodybuilding review.

14-day intake-response protocol

  1. Every morning: use the bathroom, then weigh before food/drink, in minimal clothing, on the same hard-floor scale.
  2. Log every ingredient in its stated weight state, including cooking oil, honey, milk and restaurant food.
  3. Log total calories, protein, sleep duration, training, bowel comfort and unusual high-sodium meals.
  4. Do not interpret single readings. The first valid rolling average is day 7.
  5. Compare the mean of days 1-7 with days 8-14. This is intake-response tracking, not a clean maintenance measurement.
  6. Do not cut calories because of the initial jump. Early glycogen, water, sodium, inflammation and gut content can move the scale. Waist alone also never triggers a reduction.

Reading the scale

  • Weeks 1-3: expect more noise from glycogen restoration, water carried with glycogen, sodium changes, larger gut content, training soreness and possible creatine-related intracellular water.
  • Later: trust consecutive 7-day averages. A stable multi-week slope is more useful than any narrative about one heavy morning.

3. Adherence-triggered phases

PHASE A

Appetite-limited

Entry: current state. Start at corrected 3,300 kcal.

  • Use oil, peanut butter, 2% milk, raisins, honey and white rice.
  • Drink most zero-calorie fluids between meals, not immediately before them.
  • Reduce giant salads, broth soups, bran cereal, popcorn, brown rice, huge crucifer/berry bowls and more than one cup cooked legumes in one meal.
PHASE B

Full surplus

Enter only when all are true:

  • Target hit on at least 6 of 7 days.
  • No moderate digestive complaint lasting more than two days.
  • Fibre floor hit on at least 5 of 7 days.
  • Projection remains below 190 lb.

Add one +250 module, usually Rice Drive.

PHASE C

Final drive

Enter when: Phase B intake is hit 6/7 days for two weeks, digestion is stable and projected finish is still under 190 lb.

Add a second +250 module. Review every seven days; another +250 step is allowed after a further 14 compliant days if the projection still misses 190. Calories are not capped at 3,800 merely because the estimate began lower.

Finished-batch weighing procedure - do not assume cooking yield

  1. Record raw/dry batch inputs before cooking: chicken 1,200 g RAW, rice 1,480 g DRY, lentils 390 g DRY.
  2. After cooking and brief steam release, weigh each finished cooked batch in a tared bowl.
  3. Use the formula below to portion the correct raw/dry equivalent even when water loss/gain differs.
Cooked portion weight = finished cooked batch weight x planned raw-or-dry equivalent / total raw-or-dry input Chicken portion = finished chicken weight x 100 / 1,200 Box A rice portion = finished rice weight x 100 / 1,480 Box B rice portion = finished rice weight x 130 / 1,480 Lentil portion = finished lentil weight x 30 / 390

Per-container macros can be recomputed as total batch macros x cooked portion weight / finished cooked batch weight.

Ground turkey trial - once

Turkey keema-style rice cup

10. Food safety - mandatory weekly rules

Sunday

  • Use shallow containers.
  • Close and refrigerate/freeze promptly once active steaming stops.
  • Everything enters cold storage within two hours of cooking.
  • Do not leave food out until room temperature.
  • Freeze Thursday-Friday-Saturday boxes immediately.

Daily

  • Thaw only in the refrigerator.
  • Reheat poultry/rice dishes to 74 C / 165 F internal.
  • Reheat only the portion being eaten.
  • Discard food left above refrigerator temperature for unsafe periods.
  • Cooked rice gets the same prompt cooling treatment because room-temperature rice can support Bacillus cereus.
Sunday food is not treated as Friday refrigerator food. Health Canada guidance is roughly 2-4 days for refrigerated leftovers depending on the food. The Thursday, Friday and Saturday containers go straight to the freezer on Sunday.

Mother's Sunday cooking sheet - copy this section by itself

What to cook

  • Chicken: 1.2 kg raw boneless skinless chicken thighs.
  • Rice: 1.48 kg dry jasmine rice, about 7 1/2 level cups depending on the cup.
  • Lentils: 390 g dry lentils, about 2 level cups.
  • Frozen vegetables: use the full 2.5 kg bag across the chicken boxes and turkey mixture.
  • Olive oil: weigh 231 g total for the prepared boxes and turkey mixture, about 17 tablespoons. Keep the rest for shakes/modules.
  • Turkey trial: one 400-500 g pack plain ground turkey, 100 g chopped onion and 300 g frozen vegetables.

Seasoning - no sauces or stock

Use fresh lemon, fresh garlic, plain cumin, paprika, turmeric, black pepper and measured salt. Do not use bouillon, broth cubes, Worcestershire sauce, Caesar dressing, oyster sauce, fish sauce or a mixed seasoning packet.

Order

  1. Heat oven to 220 C.
  2. Start the rice in the rice cooker. If it does not fit, cook it in two batches.
  3. Cook the lentils in the pressure cooker until tender but not mushy.
  4. Season the chicken and bake on two trays until the thickest pieces reach 74 C inside.
  5. While the chicken cooks, brown the whole pack of turkey with the onion, 300 g vegetables, 8 g olive oil and the plain spices.
  6. Steam or microwave the rest of the vegetables.
  7. Weigh the finished cooked chicken, rice and lentils separately before filling boxes. Do not guess the cooked amount.

How to divide the cooked food

Each chicken serving = finished chicken weight divided by 12 Each lentil serving = finished lentil weight divided by 13 Rice for each small lunch box = finished rice weight x 100 / 1,480 Rice for each large dinner box = finished rice weight x 130 / 1,480

Make 7 small lunch boxes, 5 regular large dinner boxes and 1 turkey dinner box. Put 175 g vegetables into each regular box. The turkey dinner gets one quarter of the turkey mixture plus 100 g extra vegetables. Add 15 g olive oil to each small box and 20 g to each regular large box. Add 18 g to the turkey dinner box.

Fridge and freezer

  • Fridge: Sunday, Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday boxes.
  • Freezer immediately: Thursday, Friday and Saturday boxes, plus the three unused turkey portions.
  • Wednesday night move Thursday boxes to the fridge. Thursday night move Friday boxes. Friday night move Saturday's box.
  • Use shallow containers. Put food into the fridge/freezer promptly once it stops steaming, always within two hours. Do not leave it out until room temperature.
  • Heat each meal until the middle reaches 74 C / 165 F.
