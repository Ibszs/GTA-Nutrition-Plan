OPENING ON A PHONE
Download START_HERE.html and the bundle, or open each linked file directly. HTML files work in Safari/Chrome and remain usable offline after download. The tracker and checklist save entries in that browser's local storage. PDF files are best for Files/Drive, printing and sharing.

WHEY LABEL STEP
Before day one, enter the exact calories, carbohydrate and fat per scoop from the physical DIESEL tub. The full plan gives the correction formula.
