Make it fit your week

Keep these meals or swap individual meals in the planner. Choose a complete menu, check its totals and shop the selected dates. Avoid adding honey to chase an old exact-calorie formula: the current food table and your labels are the useful reference.

Use the two-week review before considering calorie changes. A projected finish below 195 lb does not require extra food.

Prep once, store by the food

Rice needs prompt cooling, ideally within one hour, and a maximum 24-hour refrigerated hold before reheating, or same-day freezing for later meals. Follow the kitchen guide for the complete storage sequence. FSA rice guidance.
