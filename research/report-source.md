# Form & Fuel: evidence and implementation record

Reviewed September 5, 2026. This is the canonical source record for the current web guide, not an individualized clinical assessment or a new PDF deliverable.

Training update: the four-day training assumptions and training claim ledger below are historical. The current five-day program is documented in [the September 2026 training evidence review](training-evidence-2026.md). Nutrition and food-preparation evidence in this record remains applicable.

## Scope and working assumptions

Age 21; 6 ft 3 in; approximately 170 lb; intermediate lifting experience; four full-gym sessions of roughly 60–75 minutes; no injury reported. The user wants a wider-looking upper body, strength, familiar meals retained and richer new meals. A 195 lb goal is longer term. No 25 lb lean-tissue gain or deadline outcome is promised.

The selected starting intake is 3,500–3,600 kcal, 200–220 g protein, fat around 90–100 g with a 100 g ceiling, and carbohydrate roughly 440–480 g. Those ranges are user preferences and energy allocation choices, not measured maintenance, necessary muscle-growth thresholds or four independently additive targets. Actual complete-menu totals matter.

## Research method and stopping rule

Research was organized into two lanes: training/progression evidence and nutrition/food preparation evidence. Primary study records, journal articles, official professional guidance, Canadian government food tables, package labels and food-safety agencies were prioritized. The coordinating research pass opened the critical URLs and supplied the claim set. The guide implementation pass additionally opened the ACSM update, RIR trial, Health Canada leftovers, poultry temperatures and egg guidance, plus the redirected FSA rice document. Study limitations were retained instead of treating all sources as equal. Recipe inspiration informed original adaptations; no physical taste test was performed.

Retrieval stopped once the consequential claims had identifiable supporting sources and the main uncertainty was individual response, not a missing general citation. No current retail price verification was performed. This is targeted research supporting a practical app, not a systematic review or proof of product efficacy.

## Training claim ledger

| Decision or claim | Evidence | Limits and implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consistent resistance training and goal-specific volume | [ACSM 2026 update](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/) | Broad guidance; does not validate this exact routine. |
| More weekly sets can help, with diminishing returns | [2026 dose-response analysis, PMID 41343037](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41343037/) | No universal optimal set count. Recovery and indirect work complicate counting. |
| Most working sets can stop near failure | [Refalo et al. 2024, PMID 38393985](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393985/) | Eighteen trained participants, eight weeks, quadriceps; 1–2 RIR produced similar thickness gains with less acute fatigue than failure. Not every muscle or program was tested. |
| Add reps or load while preserving execution | [Progression trial, PMID 36199287](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36199287/) | Supports both approaches; the all-sets/top-range logger threshold is a conservative design rule. |
| Allow enough rest to sustain performance | [Rest trial, PMID 26605807](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26605807/) | Small trained-male trial comparing 1 and 3 minutes; does not establish the exact 90/150/180-second schedule. |
| Lighter work is optional fatigue management | [Deload trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10809978/) | A week of complete training cessation did not improve hypertrophy. The app's half-set week is a practical choice, not the tested intervention. |

Authoritative program data: assets/training-data.js. Sessions: Upper A Monday, Lower A Tuesday, Upper B Thursday, Lower B Saturday. Normal weekly direct sets: back 12, upper chest 8, side delts 8, rear delts 4; legs, arms, calves and abs remain included. Weeks 1–2 use about 3 RIR; later work aims for 1–2 with a conservative 2-RIR progression threshold. The lighter option uses approximately half sets and 4 RIR. Weeks 4, 8 and 12 are reviews; week 16 consolidates. No mandatory week off or forced max test.

Original program choices: four-day split, exercise order, warm-up ramp sets, rep ranges, review dates, short-session priorities, consistent equipment/load conventions and optional easy walks. These are applications of evidence, not separate study findings. The guide makes no spot-reduction claim.

## Nutrition claim ledger

| Decision or claim | Evidence | Limits and implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faster gain can add fat without consistent additional hypertrophy | [Helms et al. 2023 surplus trial](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40798-023-00651-y) | Seventeen completers, eight weeks; limited power and individual variation. Excluded anabolic-agent use; not a regimen-specific prediction. |
| More protein is not indefinitely more effective | [Morton et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2017-097608) | Mean estimated breakpoint around 1.6 g/kg/day with uncertainty, not a universal ceiling. |
| Very high protein is not a general growth requirement | [1.6 vs 3.2 g/kg trial, PMID 37516903](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37516903/) | Higher protein target is an eating preference. |
| Use a modest gain pace as a review reference | [Iraki et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.3390/sports7070154) | Narrative off-season review, not a validated personal algorithm. 0.25–0.5% weekly is about 0.4–0.85 lb at 170 lb; favour lower end here. |
| Carbohydrate fills selected energy needs | [2026 carbohydrate review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-025-02341-z) | Does not establish a mandatory high-carbohydrate hypertrophy threshold. |
| Fibre reference around 38 g/day | [Health Canada fibre](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/nutrients/fibre.html) | Reference for men 19–50; tolerance and gradual adjustment matter. |

Tracker choices: two seven-day windows, six weigh-ins per window, at least 12 intake logs total and at least 90% calorie adherence within ±10% of target before review signals. Gain below 0.25% or above 0.5% prompts review. Consider 100–150 kcal changes only after a persistent trend plus waist, recovery, intake and context review. Those thresholds are transparent operational choices, not a validated diagnostic tool. Weight projections are informational; no automatic deadline calories or cut.

## Food-safety ledger

- [Health Canada leftovers](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/general-food-safety-tips/food-safety-tips-leftovers.html): prompt shallow-container cooling, cold storage within two hours, fridge at 4°C or below, 2–3-day general leftover window, 74°C reheating.
- [FSA home food fact checker](https://www.food.gov.uk/safety-hygiene/home-food-fact-checker#rice), now redirects to [GOV.UK document](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-food-fact-checker/home-food-fact-checker): rice has stricter cooling/storage/reheat instructions; the guide uses those for rice-containing boxes.
- [Health Canada cooking temperatures](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/general-food-safety-tips/safe-internal-cooking-temperatures.html): poultry pieces and ground poultry 74°C; this is not the whole-bird temperature.
- [Health Canada eggs](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/meat-poultry-fish-seafood-safety/eggs.html): refrigerated hard-boiled eggs up to one week.

Sunday plus midweek preparation, dated containers, measured cooked-yield allocation and freezing later rice portions replace the old Sunday-to-Wednesday refrigerator map. Cooked batch × raw portion ÷ raw batch is a portion-allocation method, not a laboratory nutrient-retention calculation.

## Historical material and remaining uncertainty

Supplied PDFs remain historical copies, clearly labelled outdated in a collapsed guide section. Current web guidance supersedes their macro arithmetic, deadline-driven calorie rules and storage map. No current shopping budget is asserted; package checkout spending differs from ingredient consumption cost. Remaining unknowns include individual maintenance, actual body composition, recovery capacity, food tolerance, equipment differences, current labels and local prices. The app supports observation and revision instead of pretending these are already known.
