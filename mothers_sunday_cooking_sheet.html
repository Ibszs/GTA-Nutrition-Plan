Sunday Cooking Sheet

13 main meals + 3 frozen turkey portions. Plain instructions, no nutrition math.

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg raw boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1.48 kg dry jasmine rice, about 7 1/2 level cups
  • 390 g dry lentils, about 2 level cups
  • 2.5 kg frozen mixed vegetables
  • 231 g olive oil for prepared boxes, about 17 tablespoons
  • One 400-500 g pack plain ground turkey
  • 100 g chopped onion, 2 lemons and fresh garlic
  • Plain cumin, paprika, turmeric, black pepper and measured salt
Do not use: broth, bouillon, seasoning packets, Worcestershire, Caesar dressing, oyster sauce, fish sauce or premade marinades.

Cooking order

  1. Heat oven to 220 C. Label 13 main containers by day and A/B.
  2. Start rice. Use two batches if the cooker is too small.
  3. Pressure-cook lentils until tender but not mushy.
  4. Season chicken with lemon, garlic and the plain spices. Bake on two trays until the thickest pieces reach 74 C.
  5. At the same time, cook the whole turkey pack with onion, 300 g vegetables, 8 g oil and plain spices.
  6. Steam or microwave the remaining vegetables.
  7. Weigh the finished chicken, rice and lentils separately in tared bowls.

Division formula

Chicken serving = finished chicken weight / 12 Lentil serving = finished lentil weight / 13 Lunch rice = finished rice weight x 100 / 1,480 Dinner rice = finished rice weight x 130 / 1,480

Build the boxes

  • 7 lunch boxes: one chicken serving, one lentil serving, lunch rice, 175 g vegetables and 15 g olive oil.
  • 5 regular dinner boxes: one chicken serving, one lentil serving, dinner rice, 175 g vegetables and 20 g olive oil.
  • 1 turkey dinner: one quarter turkey mixture, one lentil serving, dinner rice, 100 g extra vegetables and 18 g olive oil.
  • Freeze the other three turkey portions in small containers.

Fridge and freezer

  • Fridge: Sunday, Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday boxes.
  • Freezer now: Thursday, Friday and Saturday boxes and three turkey portions.
  • Wednesday night thaw Thursday boxes in the fridge. Thursday night thaw Friday boxes. Friday night thaw Saturday box.
  • Use shallow containers. Cold-store promptly once food stops steaming and within two hours. Never wait for room temperature.
  • Reheat to 74 C / 165 F in the centre.
